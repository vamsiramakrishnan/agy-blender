## Visual Design Contract (from `straitjacket/docs/VISUAL-DESIGN.md`)
- **Allowed:** Semantic colour only — Blue (`#6F96FF`, bounded/addressed/controlled), Amber (`#F0B429`, identity/receipt/handle), Red (`#FF7768`, flood/loss/unanchored drift), Green (`#63D3A4`, verified outcome), Neutral (`#0D0F12` bg, `#15181D` surface, `#272C33` 1px rules, `#ECEAE3` ink). Instrument-panel layout with square corners, 1px rules, aligned baselines, uppercase display claims with tabular numerals, JetBrains Mono byte-shaped specimens, and checked-in receipts with `data-src`.
- **Forbidden:** Gradients, glow/bloom, glass/blur effects, decorative atmospheric colour, floating particles, rounded cards, and invented hashes or receipts.
