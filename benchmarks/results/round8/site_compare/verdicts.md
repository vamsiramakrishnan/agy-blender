# The two sites, judged blind

Six verdicts on the pair, three in each order, by `gemini-4-carbon` (thinking low) through `benchmarks/site_compare.py`.
The judge saw nine desktop frames of each site, at even scroll steps and 960 px wide, as "the first site" and "the second
site", with the request in `request.txt`. It never saw which run made which. `run1` is the first run's site (`../../site_run1/`),
`plugin` the second run's (`../../site_run2_plugin/`). One call was refused by a busy service and asked again.
