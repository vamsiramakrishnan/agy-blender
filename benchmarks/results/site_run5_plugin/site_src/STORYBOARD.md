# Straitjacket 3D Explainer — Storyboard

## Visual Design Rules (from `straitjacket/docs/VISUAL-DESIGN.md` & `scripts/gen_addressable_evidence_visuals.py`)

- **Allowed:** *Addressable Evidence* visual language. Dark instrument-panel aesthetic (`#0D0F12` bg, `#15181D` surface, `#1C2026` surface2, `#ECEAE3` ink, `#8D939A` muted, `#272C33` grid). Square corners, 1px rules, aligned baselines, tabular numerals, short uppercase display claims, sentence-case explanations, JetBrains Mono for byte-shaped evidence (commands, handles, coordinates, receipts). Semantic colour only: Blue `#6F96FF` (bounded/addressed), Amber `#F0B429` (identity/receipt/decision point), Red `#FF7768` (resident flood/loss/unsupported boundary), Green `#63D3A4` (verified/relocated outcome), Neutral `#8D939A`/`#272C33` (storage/structure).
- **Forbidden:** Gradients, glow, bloom, glassmorphism, decorative particles, decorative colour, rounded marketing cards, invented receipts or fabricated log lines without stating illustrative.
- **3D Material Adaptation:** Large machined prop housings use mid-value brushed steel/aluminium (`#9EA5AE`–`#B0B4B8`) and matte instrument grey (`#6E7680`) so geometry stands clearly away from the `#0D0F12`/`#15181D` floor under `NeutralToneMapping`, with dark `#1C2026` recesses and Straitjacket's exact semantic colours on working parts.

## Followed Thing

One 20,001-line integration log (`302,628` tokens, `1,002,594` B, hiding a quiet INFO needle at line `14,238`) travelling along a single calibrated evidence bench: arriving as a raw flood, entering the open Capture & Digest Press, collapsing into 11 templates + 1 quiet needle (`531` tokens) while raw bytes move into the Local Evidence Vault, re-locating across source edits in the Anchor Caliper, and passing through the seven coding-harness gate lanes.
