03 · Local store & retrieval

STORE LOCALLY, FETCH WHAT DECIDES

Captured streams stay in a local plaintext store (default limit 64 MiB per stream; unpinned artifacts eligible for ctx gc after 30 days). The agent searches the handle and pulls only the lines needed for its next edit.

ctx search run:8d8335db6848#stdout "MissingTenantError"
ctx get run:8d8335db6848#stdout --lines 1280:1300
